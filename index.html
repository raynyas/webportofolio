About Me

Hello! I'm Aria, an active vocational student programmer at SMK Negeri 1 Cianjur. My journey into tech began with the simple excitement of seeing lines of code transform into responsive, functional web pages.

I believe in learning through relentless iteration and hands-on trial. Rather than waiting to master everything before building, I prefer diving into the code, debugging real issues, and discovering how modern interfaces work from the ground up.
